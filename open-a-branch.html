Every branch runs the same way: good coaching, a safe room, and the same values we train by in London. If you think you could build that where you live, we want to hear from you.

What we look for

  • Real time on the mat — wrestling, judo, BJJ, sambo or similar.
  • Some coaching, or a clear plan for who will coach.
  • A space you can use regularly, with room for mats.
  • People around you who would turn up.

Nothing here is a hard requirement. Tell us where you are honestly and we will talk it through with you.
